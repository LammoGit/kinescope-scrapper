Lease an available job from the queue

Workers call this endpoint to declare themselves idle and available for work. If a job is ready in the coordinator's queue, it is returned immediately. If no jobs are available, the coordinator holds this connection open until a job is submitted or the internal timeout is reached.

Authorizations:
HttpMessageSignature
path Parameters
workerId
required
integer <int64> (WorkerId)
Example: 1

The unique identifier of the worker requesting a job lease.

query Parameters
timeout
integer <int32> <= 45
Default: 30
Example: timeout=30

Optional maximum time (in seconds) the coordinator should hold the connection open before timing out.
